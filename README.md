Nest Egg is a household budget site for one family keeping its money straight. The dashboard shows what is left to spend in November 2026, with income, spending and savings at a glance, a donut of where the spending went, and a month-pace strip: days left in the month, what is safe to spend each remaining day, and whether the flexible spending is ahead of or behind the plan (rent, which lands as one lump on the 1st, is left out of the pace so it never cries wolf). Transactions can be added, filtered and deleted; nine monthly budgets show progress and can have their limits changed, with an all-budgets total at the top; three savings goals track progress and take top-ups, each with a projected finish date worked out from what the household actually saves into it each month; Insights compares November with October, charts spending by week, ranks the biggest spending, and turns the household's own numbers into plain-language tips. Everything is static files — no build step, no network requests, no account — and changes are saved in the browser's local storage, with a one-click reset back to the demo month.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```
